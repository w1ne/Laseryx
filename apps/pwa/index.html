Laseryx

Laseryx is a tool for your laser cutter. It runs in your browser. You can design, plan, and cut without installing anything.

  • Design Workspace: We support SVGs and images like PNG or JPG. The app converts images to scanlines automatically. You can organize your work with layers.
  • CAM Engine: You can configure Cuts or Engraves. Save your settings for different materials like Wood or Leather. Everything happens on your computer locally.
  • Machine Control: Connect directly to your GRBL machine via USB.
  • Preview and Simulation: Visualize exactly what the laser will do before it starts.
